Questions

How does the discount work?

Once you've been a member for 3 months, you can take 10% off one job each membership year, up to £1,500 off. It's the same for monthly and yearly members.

Why the 3-month wait and the £1,500 limit?

They keep the plan fair and the price low for everyone. The plan is for people who want their home looked after all year, not a one-off discount on a single job.

Can I cancel?

You can cancel within 14 days of joining for a full refund, as long as your service hasn't happened yet. Monthly plans have a 12-month minimum because your service is included up front. After that, cancel any time. The plan terms explain exactly how it works.

Is this insurance or breakdown cover?

No. The plan covers your annual service and a discount on quoted work. Repairs are quoted separately, and members get priority booking.

Which areas do you cover?

East Grinstead and the surrounding areas. If you're not sure, give us a call.

I'm a landlord. Can I join?

Yes, one plan per property. Landlord gas safety certificates can be added at a member price. Ask us for details.
