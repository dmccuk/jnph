  • £500 setup, paid once the site is live.
  • 8% of what members pay each month, invoiced monthly.
  • £50 a month minimum while the plan is small. 8% takes over at about 36 members.
  • All member payments go straight into John's own Stripe account. I never hold his money.

What John gets

Setup (£500)

  • A new website: services, air conditioning, reviews, quote form and the Care Plan sign-up.
  • Stripe set up in John's name: monthly and yearly plans, the air con add-on, card and direct debit.
  • Care Plan terms drafted to UK consumer law, ready for a final check.
  • A launch email for John to send to his existing customers.

Ongoing (8%, £50 a month minimum)

  • Running the member list: sign-ups, cancellations, refunds, failed payments.
  • Renewal reminders sent 14 days before each renewal, as the terms and the law require.
  • Service reminders, so John knows who is due and when.
  • Website updates and hosting.
  • A short monthly report: members, revenue, renewals due, my invoice.

Benefits to John

  • Regular income. Members pay every month or year, whether or not work comes in.
  • Money upfront. Service visits are paid for before they happen.
  • Quieter months filled. Services can be booked into slow weeks.
  • More big jobs. Members come back to John for bathrooms, boilers and air con, not a competitor.
  • Air con sold to existing customers. The add-on puts his new service in front of people who already trust him.
  • No admin. I handle the payments, reminders and member questions. John does the work.

What my fee comes to

The £50 minimum only matters below about 36 members. Above that, my fee is always 8%.

Stripe fees

Stripe takes about 2–3% of each payment. This is separate from my fee and comes out before the money reaches John.

  • Standard UK card: 1.5% + 20p.
  • Bacs Direct Debit: 1%, minimum 20p.
  • Subscriptions (Stripe Billing): 0.7% on top.
  • No setup or monthly fees. (Stripe UK pricing)
