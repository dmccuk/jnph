Care Plan terms and conditions

These terms explain what you get from the JNPH Care Plan, what you pay, and how to cancel. We've kept them as short and plain as we can.

Version 0.1 (draft), October 2026.

For John to review

This is a starting draft based on common UK practice for boiler service plans and trade terms. Before it goes live:

  • Fill in the highlighted details: company number, registered address and Gas Safe number.
  • Confirm the standard (non-member) price of a boiler service and an air con service. They're used in the early cancellation rule in section 6.
  • Confirm F-Gas certification for air conditioning work. Anyone installing or servicing air con in the UK needs it.
  • Check the plan is covered by his public liability insurance.
  • Have a solicitor or a trade body (such as the Federation of Master Builders or APHC) look over the final version. This draft isn't legal advice.

1. About us

1.1 The Care Plan is provided by JNPH Limited, a company registered in England and Wales (company number [00000000]), registered office [address]. In these terms, "we" and "us" means JNPH Limited and "you" means the person who joins the plan.

1.2 We are Gas Safe registered (registration number [000000]) and OFTEC registered. You can check our registration at gassaferegister.co.uk.

1.3 You can contact us on 07912 054932 or at jnphplumbing@gmail.com.

2. What the plan includes

2.1 Each membership year, the plan includes:

  • one annual service of one gas, LPG or oil boiler at the property named on your plan;
  • 10% off one job, as explained in section 3;
  • priority booking for repairs and breakdowns, meaning we'll offer members the earliest appointment we have available;
  • a reminder before your service is due.

2.2 If you choose the air con add-on, your plan also includes one annual service of up to 3 indoor air conditioning units, and the outdoor units connected to them, at the same property.

2.3 The plan covers one property. It is for you and can't be transferred to someone else or to a different property, except with our agreement (for example, if you move house within our area).

2.4 Your membership year starts on the day your plan starts and runs for 12 months.

2.5 The plan is a servicing and discount plan. It is not insurance or breakdown cover, and it doesn't pay for repairs or parts.

3. Your member discount

3.1 You can take 10% off the price of one job each membership year, up to a maximum of £1,500 off.

3.2 You can use the discount once you've been a member for 3 months and your payments are up to date.

3.3 The job must be quoted by us in writing and accepted by you while your plan is active. The discount comes off the total quoted price, including labour and materials.

3.4 The discount can't be split across more than one job, carried over to the next membership year, exchanged for cash, or combined with any other offer.

3.5 If you cancel your plan before a discounted job has been completed and paid for, we'll charge the full quoted price for that job.

4. Payment and renewal

4.1 You can pay £18.99 a month or £199 a year. The air con add-on costs £6 a month or £69 a year. Prices include VAT where it applies.

4.2 Payments are taken by card or direct debit through Stripe, our payment provider. Monthly payments are taken on the same date each month.

4.3 Monthly plans have a minimum term of 12 months, because your service is available from the start.

4.4 Your plan renews automatically at the end of each membership year so your cover doesn't lapse. We'll email you at least 14 days before each renewal to remind you, tell you the price, and explain how to cancel.

4.5 If a payment fails, we'll let you know and try again. If a payment is still unpaid 14 days after we've told you, we may pause your benefits until it's paid.

5. Your right to cancel (14-day cooling-off period)

5.1 You can cancel your plan within 14 days of joining, for any reason, and get a full refund.

5.2 If you ask us to carry out your service within those 14 days and then cancel, we'll refund what you've paid minus the standard price of the service you've received (£[95] for a boiler service, £[85] for an air con service).

5.3 To cancel, call or email us, or use the cancellation form at the end of these terms. We'll refund you within 14 days, using the same payment method you used to join.

6. Cancelling after 14 days

6.1 Yearly plans. You can stop your plan renewing at any time. It will then end at the end of your current membership year. We don't refund part-years.

6.2 Monthly plans after 12 months. You can cancel at any time. Your plan ends at the end of the month you've paid for.

6.3 Monthly plans in the first 12 months. You can cancel, but because your benefits are available from the start, we'll ask you to pay whichever is lower:

  • the remaining monthly payments for your first 12 months; or
  • the value of what you've used (the standard price of any services you've had, plus any discount you've received) minus what you've already paid.

If you haven't used anything, you won't pay anything more.

6.4 You can cancel by phone, email, or the cancellation form. We'll confirm your cancellation in writing.

7. Booking your visits

7.1 We'll contact you to book your service, and you can also call us at any time to arrange it. We'll offer appointments on weekdays between 8am and 5pm.

7.2 Please give us at least 24 hours' notice if you need to change an appointment.

7.3 If we can't get in for a booked appointment, we'll rebook. If this happens twice without notice, we may charge a missed appointment fee of £[40].

7.4 Each service needs to happen within its membership year. If we've tried to book it and you haven't made it available, it doesn't carry over.

7.5 Please make sure the boiler and any air con units can be reached safely. Someone aged 18 or over needs to be at the property during the visit.

8. Safety and your appliances

8.1 We service appliances to the manufacturer's instructions and current industry standards.

8.2 If we find a gas appliance that's dangerous, we must follow the Gas Industry Unsafe Situations Procedure. This can mean turning the appliance off and, with your permission, disconnecting it. We'll explain what we've found and give you a written warning notice.

8.3 Your boiler must be in working order when you join. If it isn't, we'll quote for the repair before the plan's service can take place.

8.4 Some older boilers can no longer be serviced safely, or parts are no longer made for them. If this applies to yours, we'll tell you and you can cancel the plan with a refund for any service you haven't had.

8.5 Landlords: the plan's boiler service is not a Landlord Gas Safety Record (CP12). We can provide one alongside your service for an extra charge.

9. What isn't included

The plan doesn't include:

  • repairs, replacement parts, or call-out charges for breakdowns, although members get priority booking and their discount can be used on a quoted repair;
  • power flushing, descaling or other work found to be needed during a service, which we'll quote for separately;
  • more than one boiler, or more than 3 indoor air con units, unless agreed with us;
  • appliances other than your boiler (and air con units, if you have the add-on), such as gas fires and cookers;
  • damage caused by misuse, frost, or work by someone else.

10. Our work and your rights

10.1 We'll carry out all work with reasonable care and skill, as the Consumer Rights Act 2015 requires. If something isn't right, tell us and we'll put it right or, where that isn't possible, give you an appropriate refund.

10.2 Nothing in these terms affects your legal rights. For more information, contact Citizens Advice.

10.3 We are responsible for loss or damage you suffer that's a foreseeable result of us breaking these terms or not using reasonable care and skill. We're not responsible for loss that wasn't foreseeable, or for faults that were already there before our visit.

10.4 We don't exclude or limit our liability where it would be unlawful to do so, including for death or personal injury caused by our negligence.

11. When we can end the plan

11.1 We may end your plan by giving you at least 30 days' notice in writing. We'll refund any payments for the period after it ends.

11.2 We may end your plan straight away if payments are more than 30 days overdue after we've told you, or if anyone at the property is abusive or threatening to our staff.

12. Changes to the plan

12.1 We may change the price or these terms when your plan renews. We'll tell you at least 30 days before the change, and you can cancel without paying anything more if you don't want to continue.

12.2 We'll only change the terms during a membership year if the law requires it, or if the change is in your favour.

13. Your information

13.1 We use your name, contact details and address to run your plan, book your visits and send your reminders. We keep service records as required by gas safety rules. Payment details are handled by Stripe and we don't see your full card number.

13.2 We won't sell your details or share them with anyone except where needed to run your plan or where the law requires it. Our [privacy notice] explains your rights under UK data protection law.

14. Complaints

14.1 If you're unhappy, please call or email us first. We'll reply within 5 working days and aim to sort things out quickly.

14.2 If it's about gas safety, you can also contact the Gas Safe Register.

15. The legal bit

15.1 These terms are governed by the law of England and Wales. You can bring proceedings in the courts of England and Wales, or in Scotland or Northern Ireland if you live there.

15.2 If a court decides any part of these terms can't be enforced, the rest still applies.

15.3 Nobody other than you and us has any right to enforce these terms.
